Does your browser show Edmonton, Winnipeg or Havana an hour off?

Your browser carries its own copy of the world's time zone rules, separate from your computer's. This page asks it what time eight places show at one moment after their 2026 change (1 November in Canada and Cuba, 20 September in Morocco), 2026-11-15 12:00 UTC, and compares with the right answer. The check runs in your browser, and its answer isn't sent anywhere. This page has no analytics or trackers.
